Biography

I am currently a researcher at the National Supercomputing Center in Jinan, where my work focuses on high-performance interconnect networks. My research interests span RoCEv2 congestion control, long-distance RDMA transport protocols (LongCC), and digital twin network architectures. I am passionate about bridging theoretical models with real-world system implementations to improve the performance and reliability of large-scale distributed systems.
